How Do You Position Yourself in Life?

A Transformational Conversation with CRPF Officers

by Ruchi Doctor

Dear Reader,

Some rooms carry the weight of responsibility, service, and strength.

And recently, I had the honour of stepping into such a room — inside a session with CRPF officers, men and women who carry the safety of our nation on their shoulders.

But instead of only speaking about systems, mindset, or tactical skills — I began with a deeper, quieter question:

“How do you position yourself in life?”

Why This Question Matters

In a world where titles, ranks, roles, and routines define us — we often forget: positioning isn’t about the external.

It’s about how you see yourself when no one is watching.

It’s about:

  • What lens you view life through
  • How you interpret success and setbacks
  • And most importantly… how you define your role in the bigger story

What I Shared with the Officers

I said this with all the reverence I hold:

“You can position yourself as a survivor, or a creator.
As someone life is happening to — or someone who shapes meaning in every moment.
You’re not just serving the country.
You’re also serving the story you choose to live by.”

And in that moment — I saw stillness.
I saw reflection.
I saw leaders who were willing to pause and meet themselves.

Positioning Isn’t a Label — It’s an Identity

You can position yourself as:

  • A helper… or a healer.
  • A fighter… or a force for peace.
  • A person with a job… or a person with a mission.

The moment you choose your position consciously — everything around you starts to align.
Because clarity is the birthplace of command.

What I Witnessed in That Session

In those CRPF officers, I saw:

  • Strength without noise
  • Discipline without ego
  • Leadership without the need for validation

And yet — a hunger to grow deeper, live more meaningfully, and evolve beyond uniformed roles.

And that made me realize:

No matter your badge, your title, your income, or your past — you get to position yourself every single day.

A Question For You

How do you position yourself in life, today?

Are you shaping your story — or letting the story shape you?

What lens are you choosing to see through?
